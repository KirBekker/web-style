# Source index: website visual style and interface design

This index supports visual-design decisions, accessible interface review, responsive behavior, and AI-assisted website implementation. Use primary standards and design-system documentation as the operational baseline; research preprints help identify current questions and evaluation methods.

**Date rule:** each source below was published or substantively updated in 2025 or 2026. Dates are publication dates, arXiv submission dates, or stated update/release dates. Checked 2026-10-26.

**How to use this list:** use applicable standards and design-system docs as operational guidance, after checking jurisdiction and version. Research papers are discovery material; arXiv items are preprints and may not be peer reviewed.
